How I think about investing

My investing philosophy

Growth, in the spirit of Buffett — but my own way. Not copying, evolving.

🌱

Growth, my own way

Buffett-style thinking as a foundation — then adapted. A bit more risk appetite, and a real interest in smaller companies with huge potential.

🧪

Find your own method

There's no single right way to invest. You find yours by testing strategies over years — and treating it like a craft you keep improving.

🔢

Beyond the numbers

Facts and financials are non-negotiable. But the real edge is looking past them — at the story, the potential, the method.

🧠

Investing is a skill

Not luck, not a talent you're born with. A skill you learn, practice and get better at — with patience through the messy middle.
